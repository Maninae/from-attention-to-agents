From Attention to Agents

Where does the optimization happen - in the weights, or in the context?

Twelve chapters bridging the late-2019 CNN era to the September-2026 reasoning-agent frontier. Each chapter turns one lever - architecture, scale, pretraining efficiency, alignment, democratization, context, test-time compute, RL at scale, inference systems, agency, measurement - and the last chapter stacks them. Written for readers who knew CNNs in 2019 and watched attention just enter NLP.

0

Where you left off

The 2017 Transformer plus BERT and GPT collapse the NLP zoo into one architecture and one recipe: pretrain, then adapt.

 1

Scale is a strategy

GPT-3 turns prompting into a primitive, scaling laws make capability predictable, and Chinchilla catches the field undertraining its giants.

 2

Inside a modern pretraining run

Where the compute actually goes now: model-filtered data, MoE, MLA/GQA, RoPE + YaRN, FP8, Muon, mid-training annealing.

 3

Teaching it to be helpful

RLHF turns a document-completer into an assistant; Constitutional AI shows the labeler can be a model with a written rulebook.

 4

The recipe gets cheaper

DPO and its family drop the RL loop, LoRA and QLoRA drop the compute, LLaMA opens the weights, Tulu 3 walks the field back to online RL via RLVR.

 5

Steering without retraining

Freeze the weights and optimize the context: APE, OPRO, DSPy, GEPA. The two lanes converge on natural-language rewrites of prompts.

 6

Learning to think

o1 and DeepSeek-R1 bake CoT into the weights via RLVR, and inference compute becomes a knob you dial per request.

 7

Reinforcement learning grows up

GRPO's biases get named and patched (Dr. GRPO, DAPO, GSPO, CISPO, VAPO), rubric and generative rewards move past verifiers, RL infra becomes a stack.

 8

Paying for thought

Prefill vs decode, PagedAttention and continuous batching, prefix caching, speculative decoding, FP8/INT4 quantization, disaggregated serving.

 9

Learning to act

ReAct + tool use + MCP + computer use, agentic RL environments (SWE-Gym), context engineering, prompt injection as the new security problem.

 10

Measuring what we built

The 2026 eval landscape, LMArena style control, the Leaderboard Illusion, SAEs and attribution graphs, RSP / Preparedness / FSF safety frameworks.

 11

Computational cognition

Eleven levers stacked on one map, the recurring threads closed (CoT, democratization, multimodality), and a voice-tool capstone that resolves the reader's thread.
