A course · 2017 – 2026

From Attention to Agents

Where does the optimization happen - in the weights, or in the context?

Eight chapters bridging the late-2019 CNN era to the 2026 agent frontier. The field moved by discovering and turning a stack of levers - architecture, scale, alignment, context, test-time compute, agency - one chapter each. Written for readers who knew CNNs in 2019 and watched attention just enter NLP.

Alignment · 2022

Teaching it to be helpful

Alignment continued · 2023–2024

The recipe gets cheaper

DPO drops the reward model, GRPO drops the critic, LLaMA opens the weights, LoRA and QLoRA open the compute.

Context & prompt · 2022–2025

Steering without retraining

Optimize the context instead of the weights - APE, OPRO, DSPy, GEPA. The two lanes converge.

 5
Test-time compute · 2024–2025

Learning to think

o1 and DeepSeek-R1 bake chain-of-thought directly into the weights and open a new axis: think longer at inference.

 6
Agency · 2024–2026

Learning to act

Tool use and the ReAct loop give a model an environment; chatbot becomes agent.

Synthesis · 2026

Computational cognition

All five levers stacked - knowledge, behavior, steering, reasoning, agency - and applied to a real capstone build.
